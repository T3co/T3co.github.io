NPC_Mobile Senior engineering project

A WiFi-controlled IoT robot, and my final engineering project — a full embedded system with an FPGA at the center, coordinating a live camera feed, motor control, and remote sensing through a custom Android app.

  • Core controller: DE10-Lite FPGA, programmed in VHDL, handling the hierarchical logic that routes commands to every subsystem
  • Connectivity: ESP32 over WiFi for the app link, RS232 between the ESP32 and the FPGA
  • Vision: ESP32-CAM streaming live video, with object-detection driving an auto-aim laser mount on a servo
  • Sensing: LM75 temperature sensor over I2C, reported back to the app in real time
  • Data: Firebase for storing and syncing data between the robot and the app
  • Motor control: PWM through an L293D H-bridge driver for direction and speed
